Custom meal prep

Built around your diet.

Separate from our Bento del Caribe boxes. Meal prep is a weekly program set up one-on-one with Chef.

  1. Submit the meal prep order form online, or download the PDF and send it back.
  2. Get a phone call within 24 hours for your consultation: goals, dietary questions and allergy requirements.
  3. Chef sets your program, price (starting at $15–$20 per meal) and pickup or delivery schedule.

Dietary options

Covered on your phone consultation

  • Allergy awareTell Chef about any allergies up front. Your plan is built around them, from swapping sauces to leaving out shellfish, nuts or sesame.
  • Vegan friendlyPlant-based builds from rolls like the Bonzi (carrots, cucumber, avocado) plus edamame, tostones, maduros, yuca, white rice, collards and fresh salads.
  • High proteinLean proteins like chicken, shrimp, salmon, tuna and octopus to fuel training and recovery.
  • Healthy fatsSalmon and tuna bring omega-3s; avocado brings heart-friendly fats.
  • Smart carbsYuca, plantains, rice and potatoes for steady energy, scaled up or down to fit low-carb or high-carb goals.
  • Fiber and greensEdamame, collards, cucumber, asparagus, watermelon radish and salads for fiber, vitamins and crunch.

Allergy requirements are reviewed on your phone consultation against the nine major food allergens identified by the FDA: milk, eggs, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans and sesame. Our kitchen handles shellfish, fish, soy, sesame and wheat.

Our story

Minor Setback For Major Comeback

Have you ever questioned if you can begin anew after facing numerous setbacks in life and hearing others define your value?

Chef Izaya Tha HighFather chose to challenge norms with a concept that celebrates diversity in community, food and culture. Boriental Sushi is built on honesty, integrity and enjoyment, serving as a culinary link between cultures and reshaping the Japanese experience with Puerto Rican influences.
