Custom meal prep

Built around your diet.

Separate from our bento boxes. Meal prep is a weekly program set up one-on-one with Chef, starting at $15–$20 per meal.

  1. Submit the meal prep form online, or download the PDF and send it back.
  2. Get a phone call within 24 hours for your consultation: goals, dietary questions and allergy requirements.
  3. Chef sets your program, price and pickup or delivery schedule.
Fill out the meal prep formDownload the PDF form

Dietary options

Covered on your phone consultation

Allergy awareYour plan is built around your allergies, from swapping sauces to leaving out shellfish, nuts or sesame.
Vegan friendlyPlant-based builds: Bonzi roll, edamame, tostones, maduros, yuca, rice, collards and salads.
High proteinChicken, shrimp, salmon, tuna and octopus to fuel training and recovery.
Healthy fatsOmega-3s from salmon and tuna, heart-friendly fats from avocado.
Smart carbsYuca, plantains, rice and potatoes, scaled for low-carb or high-carb goals.
Fiber and greensEdamame, collards, cucumber, asparagus, radish and fresh salads.

Allergy requirements are reviewed against the nine major food allergens identified by the FDA: milk, eggs, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans and sesame. Our kitchen handles shellfish, fish, soy, sesame and wheat.
