1. Parties

This agreement is between TAZ (The Hassan Group LLC, 401 S Graham Street, Charlotte, NC 28202) ("TAZ"), and Boriental Sushi, owned and operated by   ("Client"). It starts on the date both parties sign (the "Start Date").

2. What TAZ builds

  • Website upgrade for borientalsushi.com: Client's logo, colors, photos and story kept, with a menu, order buttons and a catering request form added.
  • Online ordering: the meal prep order form and the bento order page, with orders sent to Client's phone.
  • Boriental Sushi by TAZ portal: Client's outreach portal with the first 41 verified Charlotte leads, messages written in Client's voice, a pipeline, an order desk and a service area map.
  • Strategic services outreach plan: social media DM outreach for events and hospitality, and an email campaign for offices and corporate teams from a separate sending domain.

Client supplies photos, dish names, descriptions, prices and the payment method customers use. Work starts the Monday after signing and follows the 30-day timeline in the proposal.

5. Ownership

Once the build fee is paid in full, Client owns its content: its name, logo, photos, menu, recipes, story and customer data. TAZ keeps ownership of its underlying code, templates, portal software and methods, and grants Client a license to use them for Boriental Sushi for as long as Client uses the build. The proposal, drafts and portal previews are confidential and may not be copied, screenshotted or shared outside Client's business.

6. Changes and revisions

The build includes two rounds of revisions on the website and order pages. New features beyond Section 2 are quoted separately before any work starts.

7. Ending the agreement

Either party may end this agreement with 14 days' written notice. Payments already made for completed work are not refundable, and any balance owed for work delivered up to the end date is still due. If Client is on the payment plan and stops paying, TAZ may pause the portal and outreach tools until the account is current.

8. Limits

TAZ will do the work professionally and on time but does not guarantee a specific number of orders, leads or bookings. TAZ's total responsibility under this agreement is limited to the fees Client has paid. Client is responsible for its food, pricing, permits and food-safety compliance.

9. General

This agreement is governed by the laws of North Carolina. It is the full agreement between the parties and replaces earlier proposals. Changes must be in writing and agreed by both parties.
