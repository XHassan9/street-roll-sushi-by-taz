Who gets which channel

One rule keeps the chef's own inbox and reputation clean: cold email never comes from the chef's address. It comes from a separate sending domain, and only to offices, corporate teams and apartment managers. Everyone in events and hospitality is reached on social.

EMAIL Separate Smartlead domain

  • Offices and corporate: law, accounting, banks, tech, HR and office managers
  • Apartment communities: community and lifestyle managers, leasing offices
  • Question-first, plain text, three emails over six days
  • Every reply is reviewed before it goes out

SOCIAL DM Instagram first

  • Singles events and social hosts
  • Event venues, breweries, taprooms and bars
  • Event planners, gyms and run clubs
  • Follow and comment first, DM second. No email, no cold calls

Apartments are my call: property managers work from an inbox and sit inside a company, so they ride the email track. Tell me if the chef would rather DM them and I will move them.

Email scripts (offices, corporate, apartments)

Question-first, plain text, no links or images in email 1. Edit anything. Replace {first}, {company}, {address} when the real list loads.

Send rules. Verify the list first. Plain text only. One question per email. Include the mailing address and an opt-out line in every email. Pause a mailbox if bounces climb. Every reply is drafted, reviewed, then sent.

Social DM scripts (events and hospitality)

Warm up first, then DM. Short, human, one question.

DM rules. Instagram first, Facebook Messenger for venues, LinkedIn for planners. No links in DM 1. Photo before pitch. About 20 new DMs a day per account, sent by hand, because Instagram flags automation. If there is no reply after DM 2, stop and keep engaging on their posts.

Food pricing worksheet

Prices are Izaya's call. Fill in what each item costs you to make and the sheet shows a suggested price and what is left over.

Left over = your price minus food cost, labor and packaging, before rent, fuel and other overhead. Suggested = food cost divided by the target food cost %. The 30% is a common restaurant rule of thumb, not a rule for this kitchen. Change it.

Questions to settle with Izaya

  • What is the smallest order worth a prep day?
  • How much lead time for trays, for events, for meal prep?
  • Deposit amount and cancellation rule
  • Per-person price for events (small, medium, large groups)
  • Delivery fee by drive ring, and a free-delivery threshold
  • How many events a week the kitchen can carry
  • What the free tasting tray costs and the cap per prospect
  • Anything he will not make or will not discount
